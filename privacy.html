1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how The Drawer at Dawn (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles personal data when you use the OneSpark mobile application and related services (“Service”). We aim to process personal data transparently and in accordance with laws that apply to us and to our users, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and, where applicable as relevant provisions take effect, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

India’s data-protection framework has a phased commencement schedule. We will update our notices, consent and withdrawal mechanisms, grievance handling, and other controls as additional requirements become applicable to the Service.

OneSpark is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not provide a parental-consent onboarding flow for minors.

2. Personal Data We Process

2.1 Account and Authentication Data

  • Account identifiers: user ID and email address.
  • OAuth profile data: name and profile image if supplied by your sign-in provider and made available through Supabase Authentication.
  • Authentication/session data: tokens and session information needed to keep you signed in securely.

2.2 Subscription and Trial Data

  • Plan type, subscription status, trial dates, subscription start/end dates, and payment-provider metadata.
  • Store or RevenueCat identifiers associated with a purchase or subscription, such as transaction/order identifiers and webhook event metadata.
  • Our internal RevenueCat webhook audit ledger may retain event identifiers, event type, integrity/hash metadata, and timestamps for idempotency, security, and audit purposes. When a OneSpark account is deleted, direct OneSpark user-ID links in that internal ledger are removed or anonymized.
  • We do not store your full payment-card number. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and their payment partners.

2.3 Challenge, Archive, and Preference Data

  • Quiz answers and results, mission-completion records, streak/progress information, and saved cards.
  • Settings needed to operate the Service, such as timezone and notification preferences.

2.4 Notification and Device Data

  • Expo push token, notification enable/disable status, timezone, and limited device/app metadata used for push delivery and troubleshooting.
  • Depending on platform and availability, this may include device model, operating system/platform, app version, or locale.
  • When you sign out, OneSpark attempts to unregister the push token associated with that device/account combination without disabling notification registrations belonging to your other devices.

2.5 Diagnostics and Product Analytics

  • Sentry: crash, error, and performance diagnostics. The app is configured not to send default personally identifiable information to Sentry.
  • PostHog: pseudonymous account identifiers and a limited set of app/product events used to understand service usage and improve the product. We do not intentionally send email addresses, names, article titles, URLs, free-form user text, push tokens, or authentication tokens as PostHog event properties.
  • PostHog analytics is configured to begin only after the initial legal-onboarding acknowledgement has been completed. Where applicable law requires a separate consent choice, acknowledgement of this policy alone is not treated as that separate consent.
  • Infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare, Railway, and Supabase may process IP addresses, request metadata, security logs, or similar technical information in the ordinary operation and protection of the Service.

2.6 Account Deletion Information

  • If you choose to delete your account, we may process an optional deletion reason and deletion-workflow status information.
  • We may retain a pseudonymized email-derived identifier, a former internal account identifier, and limited audit/security metadata as described in Section 6.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • Provide the Service: authenticate users, deliver account features, restore progress, save cards, and operate subscriptions.
  • Localize daily features: use timezone information to determine the correct local Challenge day and notification schedule.
  • Manage trials and prevent abuse: enforce one-time trial eligibility and reduce duplicate-trial or account abuse.
  • Notifications: send reminders only after the relevant device notification permission is granted and the feature is enabled.
  • Security and reliability: detect abuse, diagnose crashes, protect infrastructure, and maintain service availability.
  • Product improvement: use limited analytics and aggregate usage patterns to understand how the Service performs.
  • Legal and consumer obligations: keep records where applicable law requires us to do so and respond to lawful requests.
No personalized news profiling: OneSpark’s Home insights are global editions. We do not use your private reading, quiz, or mission history to create a personalized news feed or targeted advertising profile.

6. Retention and Account Deletion

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution requirements.

  • Account, challenge, archive, and settings data: generally retained while your account is active and deleted as part of account deletion, subject to the exceptions below.
  • Subscription/transaction records: retained as needed for subscription operations and applicable legal obligations. Where Korean electronic-commerce record-retention rules apply, contract or withdrawal records and payment/supply records may be retained for up to 5 years, and consumer complaint/dispute records may be retained for up to 3 years.
  • Notification tokens: retained while needed for enabled notifications. The device/account token is removed on successful sign-out unregister, and account-linked notification registrations are deleted as part of account deletion.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: retained according to our configured provider settings and only for as long as needed for security, debugging, analytics, or legal requirements.
  • Deletion workflow records: limited completed account-deletion audit records, such as workflow status, a former internal account identifier, pseudonymized email-derived identifier, optional deletion reason, attempt timestamps, and security/audit metadata, may be retained for up to 1 year after account deletion for deletion reliability, security, audit, and dispute-resolution purposes. Completed deletion records older than 1 year are automatically deleted. Trial-eligibility identifiers are subject to the separate retention described in Section 6.2.

6.1 Destruction Procedure and Method

When personal data is no longer needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or when an applicable retention period expires, we identify the affected records for destruction without undue delay unless applicable law requires or permits continued retention for a stated purpose.

  • Active electronic records: records in active OneSpark application systems are deleted or de-identified using technical methods intended to make the data no longer reasonably recoverable or usable in ordinary service operations.
  • Provider-held records: where applicable, we use available provider deletion mechanisms or APIs to request deletion or de-linking of account-associated data held by service providers.
  • Backups and logs: copies may remain temporarily in provider backups, replicated storage, or security logs until the applicable backup/log rotation or retention period expires. Such copies are not returned to ordinary active use except where reasonably necessary for disaster recovery, security, or legal obligations.
  • Legally retained records: data that must be kept for a statutory, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution period is restricted from ordinary service use to the extent reasonably practicable and is destroyed or de-identified when that retention period ends.
  • Paper records: if personal data is ever maintained in paper form, it is destroyed using a method such as shredding or secure disposal that is intended to prevent practical reconstruction.

6.2 One-Time Trial Eligibility Identifier

To enforce the one-time Premium Trial, we derive a pseudonymized cryptographic hash from a normalized account email together with a secret server-side value. We do not store your plaintext email in the trial-eligibility record itself.

This identifier is not treated as anonymous data: it is a limited pseudonymous anti-abuse record. It may be retained after account deletion for as long as reasonably necessary to enforce one-time trial eligibility and prevent repeated trial abuse, unless applicable law requires earlier deletion. It is not used for advertising, content personalization, or unrelated profiling.

6.3 Account Deletion Workflow

When an authenticated account-deletion request is processed, OneSpark deletes account-linked application data from its active application database, including notification registrations, saved cards, quiz/challenge progress, subscription state stored by OneSpark, and user settings. The OneSpark user row is then removed.

  • Supabase Authentication: the deletion workflow deletes the corresponding authentication user.
  • PostHog: the deletion workflow requests deletion of the exact matching analytics person together with associated events and recordings. Provider-side privacy deletion may be completed asynchronously after the request is accepted.
  • RevenueCat customer: the deletion workflow requests deletion of the RevenueCat customer record whose customer ID matches the OneSpark account ID. This does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription, and provider/store records may remain where required for transaction, refund, fraud-prevention, security, or legal purposes.
  • RevenueCat webhook audit ledger: direct OneSpark user-ID links in our internal webhook ledger are removed or anonymized. Limited non-user-linked event/audit metadata may remain for idempotency, security, and audit purposes.
  • Retry behavior: if an external deletion step encounters a transient failure, the deletion workflow is designed to retry rather than silently mark the external deletion complete.

Some records held independently by app stores, payment providers, or subscription providers may be subject to their own legal, transaction, refund, fraud-prevention, or platform retention obligations. If you have a privacy request concerning provider-held data that is not resolved by the normal OneSpark account-deletion workflow, contact us at contact@dawn-drawer.com and we will handle the request as required by applicable law.

6.4 What Account Deletion Does Not Do

Deleting your OneSpark account removes the account and associated app data as described above, but it does not by itself cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Store subscriptions must be cancelled through the relevant store if you want to stop future renewals.
