1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how The Drawer at Dawn (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles personal data when you use the OneSpark mobile application and related services (“Service”). We aim to process personal data transparently and in accordance with laws that apply to us and to our users, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and, where applicable as relevant provisions take effect, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

OneSpark is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not provide a parental-consent onboarding flow for minors.

2. Personal Data We Process

2.1 Account and Authentication Data

  • Account identifiers: user ID and email address.
  • OAuth profile data: name and profile image if supplied by your sign-in provider and made available through Supabase Authentication.
  • Authentication/session data: tokens and session information needed to keep you signed in securely.

2.2 Subscription and Trial Data

  • Plan type, subscription status, trial dates, subscription start/end dates, and payment-provider metadata.
  • Store or RevenueCat identifiers associated with a purchase or subscription, such as transaction/order identifiers and webhook event metadata.
  • We do not store your full payment-card number. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and their payment partners.

2.3 Challenge, Archive, and Preference Data

  • Quiz answers and results, mission-completion records, streak/progress information, and saved cards.
  • Settings needed to operate the Service, such as timezone and notification preferences.

2.4 Notification and Device Data

  • Expo push token, notification enable/disable status, timezone, and limited device/app metadata used for push delivery and troubleshooting.
  • Depending on platform and availability, this may include device model, operating system/platform, app version, or locale.

2.5 Diagnostics and Product Analytics

  • Sentry: crash, error, and performance diagnostics. The app is configured not to send default personally identifiable information to Sentry.
  • PostHog: when configured, pseudonymous account identifiers and app/product events used to understand service usage and improve the product.
  • Infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare, Railway, and Supabase may process IP addresses, request metadata, security logs, or similar technical information in the ordinary operation and protection of the Service.

2.6 Account Deletion Information

  • If you choose to delete your account, we may process an optional deletion reason and deletion-workflow status information.
  • We may retain a pseudonymized email-derived identifier and limited audit/security metadata as described in Section 6.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • Provide the Service: authenticate users, deliver account features, restore progress, save cards, and operate subscriptions.
  • Localize daily features: use timezone information to determine the correct local Challenge day and notification schedule.
  • Manage trials and prevent abuse: enforce one-time trial eligibility and reduce duplicate-trial or account abuse.
  • Notifications: send reminders only after the relevant device notification permission is granted and the feature is enabled.
  • Security and reliability: detect abuse, diagnose crashes, protect infrastructure, and maintain service availability.
  • Product improvement: use limited analytics and aggregate usage patterns to understand how the Service performs.
  • Legal and consumer obligations: keep records where applicable law requires us to do so and respond to lawful requests.
No personalized news profiling: OneSpark’s Home insights are global editions. We do not use your private reading, quiz, or mission history to create a personalized news feed or targeted advertising profile.

4. Legal Grounds

The legal ground for processing depends on the data, purpose, and law that applies. Processing may be necessary to provide a service you request, based on your consent, required by law, or permitted for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or service-improvement purposes where the applicable law allows such processing. Where a law requires separate consent for a specific activity, we will seek that consent.

We use the OpenAI API on our backend to assist with generating and reviewing OneSpark editorial content from public/editorial inputs. 6. Retention and Account Deletion

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution requirements.

  • Account, challenge, archive, and settings data: generally retained while your account is active and deleted as part of account deletion, subject to the exceptions below.
  • Subscription/transaction records: retained as needed for subscription operations and applicable legal obligations. Where Korean electronic-commerce record-retention rules apply, certain contract and payment/supply records may be retained for up to 5 years.
  • Notification tokens: retained while needed for enabled notifications and removed or disabled when no longer needed or when the account is deleted.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: retained according to our configured provider settings and only for as long as needed for security, debugging, analytics, or legal requirements.
  • Deletion workflow records: limited records such as workflow status, pseudonymized identifiers, an optional deletion reason, and security/audit metadata may be retained after account deletion for as long as reasonably necessary to complete deletion, maintain security/audit reliability, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal obligations.

6.1 One-Time Trial Eligibility Identifier

To enforce the one-time Premium Trial, we derive a pseudonymized cryptographic hash from a normalized account email together with a secret server-side value. We do not store your plaintext email in the trial-eligibility record itself.

This identifier is not treated as anonymous data: it is a limited pseudonymous anti-abuse record. It may be retained after account deletion for as long as reasonably necessary to enforce one-time trial eligibility and prevent repeated trial abuse, unless applicable law requires earlier deletion. It is not used for advertising, content personalization, or unrelated profiling.

6.2 What Account Deletion Does Not Do

Deleting your OneSpark account removes the account and associated app data as described above, but it does not by itself cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Store subscriptions must be cancelled through the relevant store if you want to stop future renewals.

Because our service providers operate across multiple countries, personal data may be processed outside your country of residence, including in the European Union, the United States, and locations used by global edge or platform providers. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, we rely on available contractual, organizational, technical, or other legally recognized safeguards appropriate to the transfer.

10. Security

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a personal-data incident occurs, we will respond and provide notifications where applicable law requires them.
