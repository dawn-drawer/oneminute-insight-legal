1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how The Drawer at Dawn (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles personal data when you use the OneSpark mobile application and related services (“Service”). We aim to process personal data transparently and in accordance with laws that apply to the Service and its users, including, where applicable as relevant provisions take effect, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

OneSpark is not commercially offered or marketed to consumers in the Republic of Korea. Availability is determined through our distribution settings and the markets in which we choose to offer the Service.

OneSpark is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not provide a parental-consent onboarding flow for minors.

2. Personal Data We Process

2.1 Account and Authentication Data

  • Account identifiers: user ID and email address.
  • OAuth profile data: name and profile image if supplied by your sign-in provider and made available through Supabase Authentication.
  • Authentication/session data: tokens and session information needed to keep you signed in securely.

2.2 Subscription and Trial Data

  • Plan type, subscription status, trial dates, subscription start/end dates, and payment-provider metadata.
  • Store or RevenueCat identifiers associated with a purchase or subscription, such as transaction/order identifiers and webhook event metadata.
  • Our internal RevenueCat webhook audit ledger may retain event identifiers, event type, integrity/hash metadata, and timestamps for idempotency, security, and audit purposes. When a OneSpark account is deleted, direct OneSpark user-ID links in that internal ledger are removed or anonymized.
  • We do not store your full payment-card number. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and their payment partners.

2.3 Challenge, Archive, Settings, and Legal Records

  • Quiz answers and results, mission-completion records, streak/progress information, and saved cards.
  • Settings needed to operate the Service, such as timezone and notification preferences.
  • Legal acknowledgement records: for signed-in accounts, we may keep the current legal-policy version acknowledged and the acknowledgement timestamp. Guest-mode acknowledgement may be stored locally on the device.
  • On-device cache: to improve loading performance and support limited offline use, OneSpark may store account-scoped copies of Challenge, Archive, subscription-status, notification-preference, and related service data in local app storage on your device.

2.4 Notification and Device Data

  • Expo push token, notification enable/disable status, timezone, and limited device/app metadata used for push delivery and troubleshooting.
  • Depending on platform and availability, this may include device model, operating system/platform, app version, or locale.
  • When you sign out, OneSpark attempts to unregister the push token associated with that device/account combination without disabling notification registrations belonging to your other devices.

2.5 Diagnostics and Product Analytics

  • Sentry: crash, error, and performance diagnostics. The app is configured not to send default personally identifiable information to Sentry.
  • PostHog: for signed-in accounts, OneSpark sends a pseudonymous internal account identifier and a limited set of product-usage events to PostHog EU Cloud in Frankfurt, Germany, to understand service usage and improve the product.
  • We do not intentionally send email addresses, names, article titles, URLs, free-form user text, push tokens, or authentication tokens as PostHog event properties.
  • Guest mode does not send OneSpark product events to PostHog.
  • On account change or sign-out, the app resets the prior PostHog identity before another signed-in account may be associated with product events.
  • Infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare, Railway, and Supabase may process IP addresses, request metadata, security logs, or similar technical information in the ordinary operation and protection of the Service.

2.6 Account Deletion Information

  • If you choose to delete your account, we may process an optional deletion reason and deletion-workflow status information.
  • We may retain a pseudonymized email-derived identifier, a former internal account identifier, and limited audit/security metadata as described in Section 6.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • Provide the Service: authenticate users, deliver account features, restore progress, save cards, and operate subscriptions.
  • Localize daily features: use timezone information to determine the correct local Challenge day and notification schedule.
  • Manage trials and prevent abuse: enforce one-time trial eligibility and reduce duplicate-trial or account abuse.
  • Notifications: send reminders only after the relevant device notification permission is granted and the feature is enabled.
  • Security and reliability: detect abuse, diagnose crashes, protect infrastructure, and maintain service availability.
  • Product improvement: use limited signed-in product analytics and aggregate usage patterns to understand how the Service performs and where it can be improved.
  • Legal and consumer obligations: keep records where applicable law requires us to do so and respond to lawful requests.
No personalized news profiling: OneSpark’s Home insights are global editions. We do not use your private reading, quiz, or mission history to create a personalized news feed or targeted advertising profile.

6. Retention and Deletion

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to provider operations, security needs, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

  • Active account/service data: generally retained while the account is active or the relevant feature requires it.
  • Deletion workflow records: limited completed account-deletion audit records may be retained for up to 1 year for deletion reliability, security, audit, and dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Backups and logs: copies may remain temporarily in provider backups, replicated storage, or security logs until the applicable rotation or retention period expires.
  • Legally retained records: records that must be kept for statutory, security, fraud-prevention, transaction, or dispute-resolution reasons may be restricted from ordinary service use and deleted or de-identified when the applicable retention period ends.

6.1 One-Time Trial Eligibility Identifier

To enforce the one-time Premium Trial, we derive a pseudonymized cryptographic hash from a normalized account email together with a secret server-side value. We do not store your plaintext email in the trial-eligibility record itself. This limited anti-abuse identifier may remain after account deletion for as long as reasonably necessary to enforce one-time trial eligibility and prevent repeated trial abuse, unless applicable law requires earlier deletion. It is not used for advertising, content personalization, or unrelated profiling.

6.2 Account Deletion Workflow

When an authenticated account-deletion request is processed, OneSpark deletes account-linked application data from its active application database, including notification registrations, saved cards, quiz/challenge progress, account-scoped legal acknowledgement records, subscription state stored by OneSpark, and user settings. The OneSpark user row is then removed.

  • Supabase Authentication: the deletion workflow deletes the corresponding authentication user.
  • PostHog: if a matching analytics person exists, the deletion workflow requests deletion of that exact matching analytics person together with associated events and recordings, if any. Provider-side privacy deletion may be completed asynchronously after the request is accepted.
  • RevenueCat customer: the deletion workflow requests deletion of the RevenueCat customer record whose customer ID matches the OneSpark account ID. This does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription, and provider/store records may remain where required for transaction, refund, fraud-prevention, security, or legal purposes.
  • RevenueCat webhook audit ledger: direct OneSpark user-ID links in our internal webhook ledger are removed or anonymized. Limited non-user-linked event/audit metadata may remain for idempotency, security, and audit purposes.
  • Retry behavior: if an external deletion step encounters a transient failure, the deletion workflow is designed to retry rather than silently mark the external deletion complete.

Deleting your OneSpark account does not by itself cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Store subscriptions must be cancelled through the relevant store if you want to stop future renewals.
